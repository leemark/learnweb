UX & Product Design

A practical product-design path grounded in evidence, clear information, honest interaction, and real-world validation.

By the end of this path

Turn an ambiguous problem into a testable product flow, then improve it with evidence instead of opinion.

Lessons

Six studio lessons

  • 01 Frame the outcome
    Separate the user’s job, the business constraint, and the behavior that would prove the design works.     55 min
  • 02 Research without theater
    Plan interviews, observation, surveys, and analytics around decisions you genuinely need to make.     75 min
  • 03 Make information findable
    Model content, vocabulary, navigation, and search around how people think—not your org chart.     70 min
  • 04 Prototype the risky part
    Choose fidelity by uncertainty. Prototype decisions and edge cases before decorating screens.     80 min
  • 05 Design systems with judgment
    Build tokens, components, content rules, states, and escape hatches that support coherent work.     75 min
  • 06 Test, synthesize, decide
    Run a five-person usability study, separate signals from anecdotes, and prioritize the next iteration.     90 min
